# Lessons

What earlier sessions in this copy got wrong, distilled so the next one does not. Read it at the
start of every session (`scripts/session-start.mjs` counts the entries).

**It starts empty.** Each session, after writing its log, holds its *What I got wrong* against this
list: a repeat adds the log's number to that lesson's *Seen in* line; a new lesson that could happen
to anyone is added below. The `keeping-agent-logs` skill says how, and when a lesson that keeps
coming back should move into a skill instead.

The lessons learned while this workspace itself was built are already in the skills —
`working-practice` and its `reference/what-goes-wrong.md` hold most of them.

## Shape of a lesson

```markdown
### <The rule, as an instruction>

<What happened, in a sentence or two, and why the rule follows from it.>

Seen in: <log numbers>
Would have been caught by: <skill, and section>
```
